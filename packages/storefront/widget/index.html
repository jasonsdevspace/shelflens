The search box on a page that is not the store's

This page has styles of its own that reach every list item, button and input. It stacks a banner over everything, clips the box the search sits in, and runs a React of its own that counts. One script puts the ShelfLens search box in the middle of all that. The box keeps its look, opens its list over the banner and out of the clipped box, and leaves this page's React alone. Type a few letters and watch the list. If this browser has searched the store before, empty the box and those searches come first.
